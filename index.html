Short version
  • This docket tracks Utah school districts: AI vendor contracts, EdTech apps and what is documented about how student data is handled.
  • Seven districts are tracked so far, with two full case files.
  • Only Tooele County School District’s Google Gemini contract has been confirmed signed. Washington County says its students do not have Gemini at school.
  • Four more districts (Weber, Jordan, Granite, Provo) appear with app counts from the BYU study. Canyons was added Oct. 18, 2026 from a KSL report.
  • A dash (—) in the table means nothing has been confirmed yet, not that nothing exists.

Updates since this was posted

News and records found online, with dates. Earlier entries are not changed.

  • Oct. 18, 2026Page redone to match the other Utah Hive Politics repos: new colors, short version, plain-words page, dated updates and reader questions. No earlier text was removed.Source: The Weber County Hive
  • Oct. 18, 2026 (record check)Tooele County School District board agendas on the Utah Public Notice Website for April 21, May 12, June 9 and Aug. 11, 2026, and the Aug. 11 minutes, list no item naming Google, Gemini, artificial intelligence or a data privacy agreement. Policy 5031 had its 1st read April 21 and 2nd read May 12, 2026. Read by text search of the posted PDFs; the July 28 work session and the meetings after Aug. 11 have not been checked yet.Source: Apr. 21 agenda, May 12 agenda, June 9 agenda, Aug. 11 agenda, Aug. 11 minutes
  • Oct. 18, 2026 (record check)The state rule R277-487 still lets a vendor meet the audit requirement with a self-assessment or an industry audit report such as SOC2 or SOC3. USBE’s rules page lists its last change as July 8, 2025, before H.B. 55 took effect July 1, 2026; no amendment was listed.Source: USBE, R277-487 rule page
  • Oct. 18, 2026Fixed the “Full case files built” count at the top. It showed 5 because it counted every district row that links to a case file; four rows share the same H.B. 55 case file. There are 2 case files.Source: The Weber County Hive
  • Oct. 7, 2026KSL reported that Canyons School District turned Gemini on for teachers, staff and high school students only, with a red-yellow-green rule for when AI may be used on assignments. Provo City School District told parents Gemini and other AI chatbots are not available on student Chromebooks while it finishes its AI policy. The Provo row and a new Canyons row in the table use this report.Source: KSL, Eric Cabrera, Oct. 7, 2026
  • Sept. 4, 2026 (record found Oct. 18, 2026)The board’s Standards and Assessment Committee gave preliminary approval to a “Utah Instructional Technology and Artificial Intelligence Model Policy” for districts to use when writing their own AI policies, and to creating an Artificial Intelligence Student Task Force. The final full-board action on both has not been checked yet.Source: USBE Board Summary, Sept. 3–4, 2026
  • Aug. 6, 2026 (record found Oct. 18, 2026)The State Board of Education approved a new rule, R277-334, Classroom Technology, to carry out H.B. 273. Per the board’s meeting summary, it requires districts and charter schools to adopt policies on technology and AI use, train educators and “ensure parent transparency and student protections.”Source: USBE Board Summary, Aug. 6, 2026
  • Aug. 18, 2026 (found Oct. 18, 2026)KSL reported on the BYU study and H.B. 55. Details are on the H.B. 55 case file.Source: KSL, Aug. 18, 2026
  • July 2, 2026 (found Oct. 18, 2026)St. George News reported that Washington County students do not have Gemini at school; staff use it under District Policy 3750. This matches the Washington County row in the table.Source: St. George News, July 2, 2026
  • June 11, 2026 (found Oct. 18, 2026)KUER reported that USBE calls Gemini “an option, not a requirement,” with a statewide data privacy agreement for districts that opt in. Details are on the Tooele case file.Source: KUER, June 11, 2026

Questions to ask, and whom to ask

Your local school boardHas our district signed on to the state’s Google Gemini agreement, and was it discussed or voted on in a public meeting? Which EdTech apps does our district use, and which have a signed data privacy agreement?
Utah State Board of Education, student data privacy teamWhich districts and charter schools have opted in to the statewide Google agreement, and is that agreement posted publicly? St. George News listed the team’s contact as privacy@schools.utah.gov.
Your state representative and senatorThe state rule (R277-487-7(5)) lets vendors meet the audit requirement with a self-assessment. Should a true outside audit be required?
